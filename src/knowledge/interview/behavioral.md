## Tell me about a challenge you faced.

One of the biggest challenges I faced was during my internship at CDAC, where I had to understand research papers and adapt existing deep learning architectures for JPEG image steganalysis.

Initially, understanding unfamiliar architectures was difficult, but by breaking the problem into smaller parts, studying the literature, and experimenting systematically, I successfully completed the project.

This experience strengthened my research and problem-solving abilities.

---

## Tell me about a failure.

During machine learning projects, I sometimes obtained poor model accuracy during the initial training.

Instead of changing algorithms immediately, I analyzed the dataset, improved preprocessing, experimented with feature engineering, and tuned hyperparameters.

The experience taught me that successful AI projects depend more on understanding data than simply choosing a sophisticated algorithm.

## Describe a time you worked in a team.

During my internship and college projects, collaboration was essential.

We regularly discussed implementation strategies, shared ideas, and helped each other solve technical challenges.

I believe good communication and knowledge sharing are key to successful teamwork.

## Describe your leadership experience.

I have demonstrated leadership both in college and at my current workplace.

In college, I anchored cultural events and helped organize Teachers' Day celebrations, farewell programs, and other institutional activities.

At my current workplace, I often assist colleagues with technical problems, explain programming concepts, and guide teammates whenever they need support.

These experiences have taught me that leadership is about helping others succeed rather than simply holding a leadership title.

## How do you handle pressure?

When working under pressure, I prioritize tasks, divide larger problems into smaller manageable steps, and focus on completing one task at a time.

I try to remain calm, communicate clearly with teammates, and avoid rushing into solutions without proper analysis.

---

## Describe a difficult technical problem.

One difficult problem I encountered involved adapting deep learning models originally designed for grayscale images to work with JPEG color images during my internship.

It required understanding research papers, modifying architectures, preprocessing datasets, and evaluating multiple experiments before achieving satisfactory results.

---

## How do you learn a new technology?

My learning process usually follows these steps:

- Understand the fundamentals.
- Read official documentation.
- Build a small project.
- Experiment independently.
- Apply it to a real-world project.
- Continue improving through practice.

I believe building projects is the fastest way to truly understand a technology.

---

## How do you resolve disagreements in a team?

I believe technical discussions should focus on finding the best solution rather than proving someone right.

I listen carefully to different viewpoints, discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each approach, and make decisions based on evidence, project requirements, and team consensus.
