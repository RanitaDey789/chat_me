# Educational Journey

My educational journey has played a significant role in shaping my technical foundation and problem-solving mindset. Rather than viewing education as a way to earn a degree, I have always treated it as an opportunity to build practical skills through projects, research, and continuous learning.

Starting with a Diploma in Computer Science & Technology and later pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science & Engineering allowed me to gradually strengthen my programming knowledge while exploring specialized fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Information Security, and Software Development.

Throughout my academic years, I focused on applying theoretical concepts through real-world projects, research publications, internships, and online certifications.

College transformed me from someone who simply enjoyed programming into someone who enjoys solving meaningful problems through technology.
