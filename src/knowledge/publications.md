# Publication 1

## Aqua Purity – Water Quality Analysis using Machine Learning

**Publication Date:** November 2025

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.46254/IN05.20250056

---

## Abstract

This research presents an intelligent water quality assessment system that applies machine learning algorithms to predict whether water is suitable for consumption based on multiple physicochemical parameters. The work aims to reduce manual analysis time while improving prediction accuracy, enabling faster and more reliable water quality assessment.

The study compares multiple supervised machine learning algorithms and evaluates their performance using standard classification metrics to identify the most effective predictive model.

---

## Research Problem

Traditional laboratory-based water quality analysis is time-consuming, resource-intensive, and requires specialized equipment. There is a need for an automated system capable of predicting water quality quickly using routinely measured water parameters.

---

## Objectives

- Develop an automated water quality prediction model.
- Compare multiple machine learning algorithms.
- Identify the best-performing classifier.
- Improve prediction accuracy through feature engineering and preprocessing.
- Support environmental monitoring and decision-making.

---
