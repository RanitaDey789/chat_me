## Tell me about a challenge you faced.

One of the biggest challenges I faced was during my internship, where I worked on adapting deep learning models originally designed for grayscale images to perform image steganalysis on JPEG color images.

The experience taught me how to analyze complex technical problems, work systematically, and remain persistent even when experiments did not initially produce the expected results.

## Tell me about a time you failed.

During several machine learning projects, I encountered situations where my initial models produced lower accuracy than expected.

Instead of treating this as a failure, I analyzed the data preprocessing steps, experimented with feature engineering, compared multiple algorithms, tuned hyperparameters, and evaluated different approaches.

Through this process, I learned that successful machine learning projects depend not only on choosing the right algorithm but also on understanding the data, evaluating models carefully, and continuously improving the solution.

This experience strengthened my analytical thinking and taught me the importance of persistence.
