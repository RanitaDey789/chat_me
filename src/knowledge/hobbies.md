# Painting

Painting has been one of my longest-standing passions and an important creative outlet.

I completed my formal art education through **Sarbabharatiya Sangeet-O-Sanskriti Parishad (SSSP)** and was awarded the prestigious **Shilpa Ratna** degree for successfully completing the program.

Through years of learning and practice, painting has taught me patience, attention to detail, observation, and creative expression.

### Skills Developed

- Creativity
- Patience
- Attention to Detail
- Observation
- Discipline
- Visual Thinking

### Personal Impact

Painting has helped me develop a calm and focused mindset. It encourages me to think creatively and approach problems from different perspectives, qualities that also benefit me in software development and research.
