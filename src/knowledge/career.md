# Career

# Career Vision

My goal is to become an AI Engineer and Software Developer who builds intelligent systems capable of solving real-world problems. I enjoy creating software that combines artificial intelligence with practical applications, particularly in areas such as education, healthcare, environmental sustainability, and automation.

I believe technology should simplify people's lives, improve decision-making, and create meaningful impact. My career is focused on developing systems that are both technically robust and useful to society.

---

# Why Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence attracted me because it goes beyond traditional software development. Instead of simply following predefined instructions, AI systems can learn from data, identify patterns, make predictions, and assist in solving complex problems.

During my undergraduate studies, I became increasingly interested in Machine Learning while working on projects related to environmental monitoring. My internship further strengthened this interest by exposing me to Deep Learning and computer vision techniques in the field of image steganalysis. These experiences showed me the potential of AI to address practical challenges across different industries.

Today, AI continues to be the field that excites me the most because it combines mathematics, programming, data analysis, and problem-solving into one discipline.

# Why Software Development?

While I enjoy research, I also enjoy building complete software systems.

I like taking an idea from concept to implementation—designing databases, developing backend logic, creating user interfaces, integrating AI models, testing applications, and deploying them for real users.

Software engineering allows me to transform research ideas into practical solutions, which is why I want to build products rather than only publish research.

# Startup or MNC?

I believe both environments offer valuable learning opportunities.

### Working in an MNC

An MNC provides exposure to structured software development processes, experienced mentors, large-scale systems, and collaboration across diverse teams. It is an excellent environment to strengthen engineering fundamentals, understand industry best practices, and work on products used by millions of users.

### Working in a Startup

Startups provide opportunities to take ownership of projects, work across multiple technologies, and learn rapidly in a fast-paced environment. They encourage creativity, innovation, and problem-solving with limited resources.

### My Preference

At the beginning of my career, I am open to both startups and established organizations. My priority is to work on meaningful projects, continue learning, and contribute to building impactful software.
